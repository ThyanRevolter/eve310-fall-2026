Menti 1. Legend hidden. One curve falls from 1.0 to 0, the other climbs from 0.3. Which is recall?

Answer
The falling one. A low threshold flags every pipe, so recall starts at 1 and can only fall as the threshold rises. Precision starts at the failure share, 0.30, and climbs.
