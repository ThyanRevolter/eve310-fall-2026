One-vs-all

Three classes, three binary models: triangle or not, circle or not, square or not. Each is a logistic regression, P = σ(w₁x₁ + w₂x₂ + b) with σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e−z). The highest probability wins. Click anywhere to place a new point.

In the first three panels each line is where its model gives P = 0.5. The fourth panel shades the plane by the largest P: its borders are ties between two models, not those lines, and at the dot all three tie, each far below 0.5, yet a point there still gets a class. The three probabilities do not add up to 1: three separate models, only their order matters. In scikit-learn, OneVsRestClassifier(LogisticRegression()) does exactly this.
