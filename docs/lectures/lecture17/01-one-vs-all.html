One-vs-all

Three classes, three binary models: triangle or not, circle or not, square or not. The highest probability wins. Click anywhere to place a new point.

The three probabilities do not add up to 1: three separate models, only their order matters. In scikit-learn, OneVsRestClassifier(LogisticRegression()) does exactly this.
