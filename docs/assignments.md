## Submission and late policy

Per the syllabus: late work is not accepted unless the instructor is notified at least 24 hours before the deadline. One extension per student per semester, one per assignment. Otherwise 10 points are deducted per late day. Grading counts the best 4 of 5 assignments if the final project is completed, or all 5 if it is not.
