Campus building water consumption - EVE 310 Lab 9
One file per building named water_XXX.csv where XXX is the three-letter building code.
Each CSV has two columns: DateTime (one row per day) and Water ( Gallons ) (that day's consumption).
Daily meter readings for 24 campus buildings between 2009 and 2017.
